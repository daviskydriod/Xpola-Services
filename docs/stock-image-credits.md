
### Additional localized imagery

The following additional photos were selected as authentic stock imagery from image-search results referencing Unsplash or Pexels, then resized and re-encoded as WebP for the site:

- `canada-corporate-tower.webp` — high-rise Canadian business-district architecture; used for the Canada homepage About section.
- `canada-port-operations.webp` — port workers coordinating beside cargo containers; used for Canada trade and portfolio contexts.
- `canada-distribution-warehouse.webp` — warehouse staff and inventory aisles; used for Canada operations, logistics, and portfolio contexts.
- `canada-community-volunteers.webp` — community volunteers coordinating donations; used for the Canada Community service page and related portfolio context.
- `nigeria-truck-logistics.webp` — Nigerian dump truck on an urban transport route; used for the Nigeria Logistics service page.

The original downloaded files are not redistributed; only optimized WebP derivatives are stored in `src/assets/stock-sources/`.

### Shop marketplace imagery

The shop hero promotions use three optimized photos: an electronics shop photo found through Unsplash, a hardware-store photo from Pexels, and a Nigeria-market photo found through Unsplash. These are contextual promo images only; product data, prices, and inventory continue to come from the catalogue API. Sources: [Unsplash electronics-store photos](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/electronics-store), [Pexels hardware-store photos](https://www.pexels.com/search/hardware%20store/), and [Unsplash Nigeria-market photos](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/nigeria-market).
